Cresco Scientiam is DPS Megacity's annual science fest for senior students across the city, designed to sharpen logical thinking without losing sight of imagination. Hosted every year by Delhi Public School Megacity, Kolkata, it runs on a simple belief: science taught only from a textbook is science half-learned.

Across its five editions, the fest has widened from a single afternoon of four events to a full two-day arena of sixteen — spanning chemistry, mathematics, forensics, genetics, architecture, debate, drama, music and more. What hasn't changed is the format: real problems, real deadlines, real judges.

This year's theme, asks delegates to think past the immediate brief — to treat every event not as a task to finish, but as a frontier to push. Three theme-worlds — Mythology, Cosmos, Environment — hold the sixteen events together.

Our School

Delhi Public School Megacity

Delhi Public School Megacity is built around a straightforward idea: academic excellence and genuine curiosity aren't in tension, they reinforce each other. Spread across a vast, purpose-built campus in Kolkata, the school pairs a rigorous CISCE curriculum with dedicated labs, studios, and sports facilities — and a culture that pushes students well beyond the syllabus. Its science, robotics, and debate circuits regularly send delegates to city and national-level competitions, and Cresco Scientiam is the stage where that same spirit opens its doors to every other school in the city, once a year, for two days.
